# Task 02 – User Research & Accessibility Audit

## EdVyro UI/UX Design Internship

### Project

**Student Support Service**

### Objective

The objective of this task is to understand student needs, identify usability and accessibility problems, and convert research findings into a focused UI/UX design problem.

## Target Users

The primary users are college students who use digital platforms for:

* Academic tasks
* Internship activities
* Learning resources
* Assignment submissions
* Progress tracking
* Feedback

## Research Method

A documented heuristic review was conducted focusing on:

* Navigation
* Readability
* Contrast
* Forms
* Mobile usability
* Feedback
* Progress visibility
* Accessibility

## Research Questions

1. How do you currently find important college or internship information?
2. What is the most frustrating part of using a student portal or learning platform?
3. Have you ever had difficulty finding a particular option or piece of information?
4. What problems have you experienced while using student websites or apps on your mobile phone?
5. What feature would make a student-support platform easier and faster to use?

## Key Findings

### 1. Navigation

Important actions should be easy to locate without searching through multiple menus.

### 2. Readability

Long blocks of information can make instructions difficult to scan.

### 3. Contrast

Low-contrast text can reduce readability and create accessibility barriers.

### 4. Forms

Submission forms should clearly communicate required fields, accepted file types, errors, and success states.

### 5. Mobile Usability

The experience should be designed mobile-first because students may primarily access the platform through smartphones.

## Main Pain Points

* Difficult navigation
* Dense information
* Unclear submission status
* Poor mobile usability
* Unclear error messages
* Weak progress visibility

## Design Problem

College students need a simple, accessible, and mobile-friendly student-support platform that helps them quickly understand assigned tasks, find resources, submit proof, and recover from errors.

## How Might We?

**How might we design a student-support experience that helps students complete their assigned tasks quickly, confidently, and independently?**

## Success Criteria

* Users can locate a required task within 30 seconds.
* Users can understand the task objective and deliverable without assistance.
* Users can complete a submission without confusion.
* Users can understand and recover from errors.
* The primary journey works effectively on mobile.
* Text and controls follow appropriate accessibility and contrast considerations.

## Design Direction

The research findings will be used to create:

* User flow
* Sitemap
* Screen inventory
* Mobile-first wireframes
* Desktop adaptation
* Error states
* Success states
* Progress indicators
